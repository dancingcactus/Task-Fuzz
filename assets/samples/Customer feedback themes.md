# Customer feedback themes

From the #report-builder thread and support ticket 4821, for Marcus. Three themes came up again and again.

## 1. Big tables are slow

Seven customers mentioned it, more than anything else.

> "Anything over 50k rows and the builder just sits there." — Hana, Northwind

- Loads stall past **50,000 rows**
- Sorting a large table freezes the page
- Two teams export to a spreadsheet to work around it

## 2. Saved views get lost

| Customer | What happened | Seen |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Northwind | Filters reset after a refresh | 3 times |
| Fabrikam | A shared view opened blank | 1 time |
| Contoso | Column order lost on export | 2 times |

## 3. People want scheduled exports

Four customers asked for a weekly export by email. Nobody asked for more chart types.
