## Design Specificity Verdict
Character is product-specific (Fuzz perch, stamp/drop, card into Fuzz, grow-only bar, "past its time"); the card's structure is generic (six uppercase micro-labels, boxed callouts, tab row). Personality sits on the card rather than organising it.
Detector: CLI 1 finding (cream-palette, light --bg #FBF3E8, index.html). Browser: Triage bounce-easing x5 (css/fuzz.css:23,149,156), dark-glow on .btn.primary (css/app.css:217), nested-cards .firststep (css/app.css:274); Capture low-contrast placeholder 3.9:1 (no ::placeholder rule); bounce-easing on BODY in Lineup/Capture (attribution artifact). cream-palette and Fuzz bounce are sanctioned brand (Warm Theme System, Fuzz canon).

## Priority Issues
- [P1] Decision sits below the actions (js/view-triage.js:254-265): instructions -> 5 buttons -> "The one thing to decide" -> title. Fix: ask first in display size, title secondary, then instruction, then actions; or Fuzz's proposed outcome + one Yes. Note README principle 13 pins actions at top deliberately. -> /impeccable layout
- [P1] High-stakes moments get the least ceremony: Approve and send buried in the dialog, no recipient/channel, no edit, fake Undo after Sent (js/view-tasks.js:352,386-390,408). Fix: pin draft + actions, "To Marcus · Slack #offsite-planning", Edit draft, delayed send window, own Fuzz reaction. -> /impeccable shape / harden
- [P1] Triage slow and noisy, including for screen readers: lock() 2-3s (view-triage.js:41-51), no action keys, every quip in aria-live (view-triage.js:128), rush repeats every 30s (329-341). Fix: unlock on render, A/D/S/P keys + legend, separate live region for outcomes only, rush lines once. -> /impeccable harden
- [P2] Choice overload where users are overloaded: Overloaded check-in 8 options with "Hold the rest" primary; stale card shows two decision sets; Schedule 8 options. Fix: collapse moods after pick, "Quick ones first" primary, hide actions until keep, 3 tiles + More. -> /impeccable distill
- [P2] Inconsistency and leftover dev UI: 3 secondary-button styles, icon-label spacing, duplicate Entity check labels, orphan "·", mood lab under every card, popovers persist across routes, Escape scope, FAB/dock covering content on mobile, dark-mode accent shadow, nested .firststep box, placeholder contrast. -> /impeccable polish
