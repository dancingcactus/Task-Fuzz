What to look at

  • Slow motion is the fastest way to see it. Turn it on for Cheer, Shrug and Point, and watch the hips start before the arms and the arms before the head.
  • Stillness. Before never stops moving. After gets somewhere and stays there, with only breathing underneath.
  • Cheer: a dip, then a rise that slows at the top and a fall that speeds up, then a landing that sinks into the knees. The old one bounces off the floor at full speed.
  • Wave: the wrist trails the arm, and there is a pause between waves.
  • Rushing: four key poses, down and up on each foot, instead of two sine waves.
  • Sit & doze: Fuzz hops onto the top edge of the card, and the legs settle and hang still. The nod is one slow sink and one fast catch, which a sine wave can’t do.
  • Bored: old tape: only After knows this one. It plays once, then goes back to what Fuzz was doing.
  • Power off: the head drops first, then the legs sway to a stop. Pick another reaction and Fuzz hops back up. Boop it while it’s seated and it stays seated.
  • Switching: click reactions quickly. A new one starts from wherever Fuzz is.
  • Calm: After does the gesture once, then holds still. Before ignores the setting below the neck.
