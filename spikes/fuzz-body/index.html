What to look at

  • Does the body say more than the face? Pointing at a task, a shrug, hands on hips and arms crossed carry meaning the face alone can’t.
  • Does it fight the face? At perch size the screen face is the main signal. Watch whether busy limbs (rushing, dancing) pull the eye away from it.
  • Toggle “Show body” on the big Fuzz to flip between the two versions mid-reaction.
  • Space: the triage card would sit 60–80 px lower, or the face gets smaller. The small logo marks and notice faces stay head-only either way.
  • Calm setting: the head already turns down its motion; the limbs here don’t yet, and would need to.
  • Layering: raised arms (cheer, wave, antenna flick) swap behind the head so they don’t cross the screen; hands near the face (chin, scratch) stay in front.
  • Cost: limbs are three paths each, redrawn per frame with JavaScript (the head is pure CSS). Fine for one Fuzz on screen.
